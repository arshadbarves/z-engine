How large and risky is the work this request to a coding agent asks for? Judge the whole request: how many files and parts of the project it will likely change, and whether a mistake would be hard to undo.

- small: A question, an explanation, a lookup, or a change in one place, such as one function or one file.
- medium: A focused change to a few related files with a clear approach.
- large: A change across many files or parts of the project, a new feature, a migration, a rewrite or refactor, or anything risky to get wrong.
